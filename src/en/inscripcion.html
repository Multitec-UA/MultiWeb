Sign-up and membership

Join the most active tech community at the University of Alicante. One fee a year and you are in.

Membership fee

12 €a year

A yearly fee that keeps the association running and growing.

Subscribe Manage my subscription

You can cancel your subscription whenever you want.

Board approval

To finish signing up, the board has to approve your application.

It is done by hand, so it can take up to 48 hours. Bear with us.

* If it is not approved, you are refunded automatically.

What membership gets you

  • A member's T-shirt, to pick up at the Multicasa
  • An @multitecua.com account, with 1 TB of Google Drive
  • Claude Max at half price, through the association’s agreement with Anthropic (claude.multitecua.com)
  • Access to the Multicasa: coworking, 3D printing, electronics, games
  • The association’s Minecraft server, members only
  • Access to the internal projects
  • The WhatsApp groups, and the people in them
  • A voice and a vote at assemblies and elections
  • The option to book rooms at the EPS
  • Access to any of the association's resources
  • Members-only events and training
  1. Pay the feeBy card, on the sign-up page. One minute.
  2. The board approves youIt is a manual step and can take up to 48 hours. If it is not approved, the money comes back on its own.
  3. Get your welcomeYour @multitecua.com account, the groups and the welcome pack: T-shirt, pen and wristband.
